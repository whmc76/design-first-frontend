# Creative Coverage

Use this reference before creating polished visuals. It determines the smallest artifact set that can govern the requested video and graphic package without pretending every permutation needs a separate design.

## 1. Build the Deliverable Graph

Record the real production surfaces:

- Video package: opener/title sequence, sting/bumper, segment intro, chapter card, transition, lower third, subtitle/caption treatment, quote/data card, watermark/bug, CTA, end card, credits, thumbnail, and promo cutdown when scoped.
- Graphic package: key art, poster, cover, thumbnail, carousel, social post/story, promo card, schedule/program tile, data graphic, print piece, and other requested applications.
- Format axes: aspect ratio, resolution/size, duration, platform/placement, viewing distance, language/script, light/dark/busy imagery, and print/digital delivery.
- Timeline/state axes: entry, reveal, hold, update, exit, transition, success/failure, live/recorded, missing media, and localization or content extremes.

Do not invent deliverables that were not requested or implied by an existing production system. Do not treat every crop or copy change as a separate design family.

## 2. Create the Coverage Matrix

```markdown
| Coverage ID | Medium | Deliverable/application | Platform/placement | Format/duration | Content/language condition | Master/template family | Artifact ID | Output proof | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C-01 | video | program opener | main episodes | 16:9 / scoped duration | primary language | opening system | A-01 | rendered sequence | APPROVED |
| C-02 | video | lower third | episodes + clips | 16:9 and vertical adaptation | longest real name/title | information overlay | A-02 | motion template sample | PLANNED |
| C-03 | static | episode thumbnail | channel grid | platform specification | bright and dark footage | promo master | A-03 | exported variants | PLANNED |
```

Allowed planning statuses: `PLANNED`, `APPROVED`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, `BLOCKED`. A blocker makes the package incomplete.

## 3. Group into Production Families

One master/template may cover several rows when the meaningful decisions remain the same. Create a distinct artifact or proof when a row changes:

- Content hierarchy or communication job.
- Composition/template structure rather than only crop or copy.
- Timeline role, transition grammar, or motion behavior.
- Subtitle/caption accessibility or delivery mode.
- Viewing scale, aspect ratio, or safe region enough to change hierarchy.
- Language/script, text expansion, or direction enough to change typography/layout.
- Footage/image condition enough to require a different contrast or placement strategy.
- Editable/template behavior, alpha/compositing, print production, or export pipeline.

Use annotations or parameter rules when only ordinary values, colorways, episode numbers, or minor copy change.

## 4. Plan the Artifact Set

```markdown
| Artifact ID | Coverage IDs | Type | Governing decisions | Source/reference | Editable source | Approval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A-01 | C-01, C-04 | system board + master frames | identity, type, palette, motif, image treatment | brand + real assets | source project | pending |
| A-02 | C-02 | lower-third stress frames + motion proof | long names, contrast, entry/exit, edit controls | A-01 | template project | pending |
| A-03 | C-03 | graphic master + adaptations | crop, hierarchy, small preview | A-01 | layered source | pending |
```

The artifact set may combine supplied brand guidelines, mood references, system boards, master styleframes, storyboards, animatics, subtitle stress frames, template proofs, layered masters, and rendered samples. A moodboard or contact sheet is orientation, not implementation authority.

A single styleframe is sufficient only when the matrix has one production family, one output condition, and no timeline, adaptation, subtitle, or delivery question requiring separate evidence. Record the exception.

## 5. Coverage Gate

Do not freeze the production contract or multiply assets until:

- Every scoped deliverable and material format/language/timeline condition has a coverage row.
- Each row maps to an approved governing artifact and a planned output proof, or is deliberately `OUT_OF_SCOPE`.
- Shared system decisions remain consistent across masters and adaptations.
- Distinct families are not hidden inside one unreadable board or brittle universal template.
- Primary content extremes, busy/quiet imagery, and smallest/most constrained placements have evidence.
- Rights/source, editability, production owner, and delivery requirements are known for primary outputs.

No `PLANNED` or `BLOCKED` row may be presented as complete.
