# Video and Graphic Production Contract

Create this contract before broad asset production. Keep platform-specific values tied to the real brief or current authoritative specification; do not invent universal export settings.

## 1. Authority and Scope

- Approved brief, brand sources, creative references, and supersession order.
- Coverage matrix and artifact IDs.
- Deliverable families, platforms/placements, formats, durations, languages, and explicit non-goals.
- Protected brand elements, content truth, accessibility/legal obligations, and production constraints.
- Approval owner and what requires renewed approval.

## 2. Creative System

- Audience, message, tone, program/channel/campaign identity, and recognition target.
- Logo/lockup rules, clear space, placement logic, and monochrome/small-scale behavior.
- Typography roles, hierarchy, numeric/name/data treatment, and script/font coverage.
- Palette, semantic colors, contrast strategy, and behavior over light/dark/busy imagery.
- Graphic motif, layout grammar, image/footage treatment, icon/illustration/data style, texture/depth, and density.
- Cross-format invariants and deliberate variations.

## 3. Video Package Grammar

```markdown
| Element | Timeline job | Content hierarchy | Entry/hold/exit | Background/footage relation | Audio relation | Editable parameters | Coverage/artifact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lower third | identify speaker | name, role, organization | specified by motion proof | readable on busy footage | optional cue | text, color role, duration, side | C-02 / A-02 |
```

Cover only scoped elements such as opener, sting, transition, chapter, lower third, subtitle/caption, quote/data card, bug, CTA, end card, and credits. State how elements coexist and which has visual priority.

## 4. Graphic Application Grammar

```markdown
| Family | Communication job | Master hierarchy | Adaptation logic | Image/crop rule | Copy extremes | Production source | Coverage/artifact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

Define which applications share a master and which require their own structure. Record small-preview behavior, crop/focal rules, print/digital differences, editable fields, and what may be removed when space contracts.

## 5. Type, Subtitle, and Localization Contract

Reference `subtitles-and-type.md`. Record:

- Text classes and which are accessible captions versus editorial/decorative graphics.
- Font source/license, script coverage, fallback, hierarchy, spacing, case, punctuation, emphasis, and background treatment.
- Line-breaking, timing, safe placement, speaker/SDH notation, sidecar/burn-in modes, and localization ownership.
- Longest/shortest real examples, language expansion, RTL or mixed-script behavior, and missing-glyph fallback when relevant.

## 6. Motion and Audio Contract

Reference `motion-packaging.md`. Record triggers/timeline moments, purpose, elements/properties, duration class, easing/rhythm, cut/transition relationship, loop/interruption behavior, audio cue/sync, quiet version, and fallback/static result.

## 7. Assets, Rights, and Provenance

```markdown
| Asset | Source/owner | Rights/territory/term | Allowed transformations | Required credit/legal | Missing/failure fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

Include logos, fonts, photos, footage, music, sound effects, voice/talent likeness, illustrations, stock, generated media, data, and client-supplied materials as applicable. Unknown rights block final delivery.

## 8. Production and Template Contract

- Actual editing/design/compositing/caption tools and handoff versions when relevant.
- Master/source project ownership, naming, folder structure, linked/embedded asset rules, and editable controls.
- Template inputs, limits, responsive/adaptive rules, overflow behavior, localization path, and failure states.
- Color management, alpha/compositing, media relink, proxy/full-resolution, font availability, and plugin/dependency assumptions.
- Render/export owner and reproducible steps or preset source.

## 9. Delivery Matrix

```markdown
| Deliverable ID | Coverage IDs | File/use | Dimensions/aspect | Duration/frame rate | Color/alpha | Codec/container or format | Audio | Captions | Print/platform requirements | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

Populate from the real platform, broadcaster, printer, client, or repository specification. If a requirement is unknown, mark it unknown and stop short of delivery claims.

## 10. Preserve, Remove, and Deviate

- Must preserve: brand recognition, content/legal truth, accessibility, editability, and required production behavior.
- Must remove: obsolete masters, contradictory variants, unlicensed assets, fake sample data, broken links, and superseded exports when safe and authorized.
- Known deviations: source expectation, production constraint, approved resolution, and updated evidence.

## Freeze Gate

Freeze only when coverage is complete, primary content/assets and rights are known, the creative system governs every family, subtitle/type and motion rules are explicit where relevant, risky template/render behavior is proven, and every delivery row has planned acceptance evidence.
